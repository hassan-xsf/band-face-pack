Install it

Downloads started. If your browser asks to allow multiple downloads, say yes.
  1. Get Gadgetbridge on your Android phone and pair your watch (needs its auth key once: step-by-step guide).
  2. Open each .face file with Gadgetbridge and tap Install.
  3. On the watch, long-press the watchface, swipe to the new one and tap it.
  4. For apps: open the installer face on the band, tap Install, then switch the app On in Settings → Manage apps.
Tip: open this page on your phone and the files land right where Gadgetbridge can open them.
