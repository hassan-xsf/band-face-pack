1

Pair with Gadgetbridge

Get Gadgetbridge on Android and add your watch with its auth key. One time only: step-by-step guide.

2

Open the .face

Tap each downloaded .face file, open it with Gadgetbridge and press Install.

3

Switch to it

On the watch, long-press the watchface, swipe to the new one and tap it. For apps: switch to the installer, tap Install, then turn the app On in Settings → Manage apps. After the band restarts, install it again.
